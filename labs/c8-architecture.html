DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Read the arrows: left → right across the top, down to RAM, then left through external storage and recall. The lower return arc loads selected patterns back into RAM on demand; the short reverse arrow supplies inference. These are connected loops, not a mandatory six-step sequence: inference is optional, disk flush is capacity-triggered, and recall is context-triggered. The dashed recall card shows a proposed composition of operations; no end-to-end disk-paging benchmark is supplied. Toy pattern keys and similarity bars are illustrative, not measured results. Shared atomic encoding and consistent merge semantics are prerequisites for distributed contributions.
